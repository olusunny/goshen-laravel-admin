Try the agreed behaviour.

These controls simulate the server and connection. They are outside the proposed mobile interface.

Test offline deactivation
  1. Leave the book downloaded.
  2. Turn “Phone online” off.
  3. Turn “Add-on active” off: reading continues.
  4. Turn “Phone online” on: reading becomes unavailable.
  5. Reactivate: your saved hymns remain.

Prototype simulations only. No connection to production, no real download and no account changes.
